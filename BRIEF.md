# TechVerse Solution — Project Brief

## Business Overview
TechVerse Solution helps small and medium businesses build a stronger online presence — starting with websites, but not limited to them. The business also offers web application development, integrations/automation, hosting & maintenance, and legacy application modernization.

- **Business model:** Hybrid — one-time build fees + optional monthly hosting/maintenance subscriptions
- **Stage:** Side gig for now, with intent to scale to full-time in a few years as clientele builds
- **Pricing strategy:** Priced at the lower end initially to win clients, build a portfolio, and undercut competition. Pricing can rise later as reputation grows.
- **Founder background:** Real, hands-on experience in software architecture, full-stack development (.NET/C#, Azure, Blazor, microservices, Kubernetes, OpenShift), and enterprise-grade systems — applied at SMB scale and pricing. (Do not reference any previously closed business.)

## Core Positioning
"Helping small and medium businesses build a stronger, more capable online presence" — websites are the starting point, not the ceiling. Avoid technical jargon; speak to outcomes (credibility, being found online, saving time) since the audience is non-technical business owners.

## Services Offered
1. **Website Design & Development** — single-page and multi-page (3–5 page) websites, mobile-friendly, custom-built (not template-generic)
2. **Web Application Development** — client portals, booking systems, internal tools/dashboards, custom scalable features
3. **Hosting & Maintenance Plans** — subscription tiers (see pricing)
4. **Integrations & Automation** — payment processing, CRM/booking tool connections, workflow automation, AI-powered tools
5. **Website & Platform Modernization** — migrating from outdated platforms, performance improvements, redesigns
6. **Native Application to Cloud Migration** — moving legacy desktop/on-prem apps to modern cloud/web platforms; reduces infrastructure dependency, improves accessibility and lowers long-term maintenance cost
